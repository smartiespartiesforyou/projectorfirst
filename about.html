THE STORY BEHIND PROJECTOR FIRST

Built from real experience in senior living.

Projector First was created to make group activities easier to run and easier for residents to see, follow, and enjoy. The focus is simple: big text, clear screens, familiar ideas, and games that work well on a TV or projector.

Built by a Former Activity Director

Projector First comes from firsthand experience planning and leading activities in long-term care. Activity departments need tools that are ready when the room is ready — without complicated setup, tiny controls, or a long learning curve.

The Thinking Behind the Design

A background in Psychology and years of working with seniors shape the way each activity is designed. Readability, attention, memory, conversation, and confidence all matter. The goal is not simply to put a game on a screen. It is to make the activity easier for the whole room to participate in.

Our Mission

To create large-screen activities that feel natural, accessible, and enjoyable for senior living communities — while making the Activity Director's job a little easier.

Accessibility at the Core

Projector First is designed with older adults and group viewing in mind. Games use large text, strong contrast, simple navigation, and clear visual feedback. Many activities are intentionally designed so residents can participate by calling out answers while one person runs the screen.

The site is developed with WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility principles in mind and continues to improve as the library grows.

If you encounter an accessibility issue or have a suggestion, email helloprojectorfirst@gmail.com.
