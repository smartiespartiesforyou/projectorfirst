Legal & Policies

The policies below explain how Projector First handles privacy, subscriptions, refunds, and accessibility.

Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. ProjectorFirst does not sell or share your personal data. Payment information is handled securely by Stripe. We collect only the information necessary to manage your subscription and provide technical support.

Terms of Service

By subscribing to ProjectorFirst, you agree not to redistribute, copy, or modify the apps for resale or use outside your facility. Subscriptions renew monthly unless canceled before the next billing cycle.

Refund Policy

Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis. If you experience a technical issue that prevents normal use, please contact helloprojectorfirst@gmail.com within 7 days of purchase for assistance.

Accessibility Statement

We strive to make all apps readable, high-contrast, and screen-friendly for seniors and caregivers. If you encounter an accessibility issue, please let us know so we can improve.
